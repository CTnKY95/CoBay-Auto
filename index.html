Meet the Mechanic

Built on Grease
and Honesty

I'm Chaz Bass — a certified ASE Technician with 20+ years of experience wrenching on everything from daily drivers to weekend project cars. I started CoBay to make vehicle maintenance less intimidating and more accessible. The goal is to help people save money, understand their vehicles, and build confidence by learning skills they may have been afraid to try alone. I also wanted to create a community around DIY automotive repairs..

When I show up at your vehicle, you get full transparency. I'll walk you through what's wrong, why it matters, and exactly what it'll cost before I touch a bolt. No upsells, no phantom fees.

I cover a 25-mile radius of Rincon, GA and bring a fully stocked service van — diagnostic equipment, specialty tools, and the most common parts already on board.
