  [13] CO-REPAIR PROGRAM
================================================================================

  What Is Co-Repair?
    A guided repair experience where the customer learns alongside the
    technician. Instead of simply dropping off the vehicle mentally and
    waiting, the customer gets explanation, guidance, and the option to
    participate safely during eligible services.

  Why It's Offered:
    To build confidence, transparency, and community by helping people
    learn skills they may normally be afraid to try by themselves.

  Best For:
    Beginners, DIY-curious vehicle owners, parents/teens, cost-conscious
    customers, hands-on learners, and anyone who wants to understand what
    is being done to their vehicle.

  What Customers Walk Away With:
    A better understanding of their vehicle, the repair performed, the
    tools used, basic safety principles, and what maintenance may be
    needed next.


================================================================================
  [14] CO-REPAIR: HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

  Step 1 — Booking / Initial Contact:
    Customer requests service online/by phone/text and chooses a standard
    repair or asks about Co-Repair. Vehicle details, location, symptoms,
    and goals are reviewed before confirming.

  Step 2 — Diagnosis:
    When diagnosis is needed, findings are walked through — explaining
    likely causes, showing what is being checked, and discussing options
    before repair work begins.

  Step 3 — Repair / Teaching:
    During eligible repairs, customers may watch, ask questions, and help
    with safe tasks when appropriate. The technician remains responsible
    for the repair process, safety, and final decisions.

  Step 4 — Final Walkthrough:
    Review what was done, what was learned, any parts replaced, what to
    watch for, and recommended next steps.

  Step 5 — Follow-Up / Written Guide:
    Customer may receive a simple follow-up summary, checklist, parts/tool
    notes, or maintenance recommendations depending on the service.

================================================================================
  [19] CO-REPAIR: FAQ
================================================================================

  Q: Do customers need mechanical experience?
  A: No. Co-Repair is designed for people who want to learn at a
     comfortable pace.

  Q: Can they watch instead of helping?
  A: Yes. Customers can choose to watch, ask questions, and learn
     without physically helping.

  Q: Can they bring a friend, spouse, or teenager?
  A: Yes, with advance approval. One additional person maximum. Minors
     must be accompanied by a parent/guardian.

  Q: Is Co-Repair available for every repair?
  A: No. Co-Repair is only available for repairs that are safe, practical,
     and appropriate for a mobile setting.

  Q: Will it take longer than a normal repair?
  A: Yes. Time is built in for teaching, questions, and walkthroughs.
     Typically twice the standard labor time.

  Q: What should customers have ready before you arrive?
  A: Vehicle accessible, keys available, safe space around the vehicle,
     known history/symptoms ready, parts on hand if customer-supplied,
     and permission to work at the location.

  Q: Do customers need their own tools?
  A: No. CoBay provides all tools needed. Customers can request a
     beginner tool list if interested.

  Q: Can customers record the session?
  A: Recording may be allowed with permission, as long as it does not
     interrupt safety or the repair process.
