Learn to Read Your Vehicle

A big part of every Co-Repair session is teaching you to recognize and diagnose common problems before they become expensive ones.

Sounds & Symptoms

Common Questions

Co-Repair FAQ

Do I need any mechanical experience?

None at all. Co-Repair is designed for complete beginners. I'll explain every term, every tool, and every step from scratch.

Is it available on every repair?

Most repairs qualify. The only exceptions are jobs too complex or confined to explain safely while working. I'll let you know when you call.

What's the extra cost?

Co-Repair adds $40/hr to the standard repair rate for teaching time. Most sessions add 30–90 minutes depending on the repair.

What do I walk away with?

A completed repair, a written step-by-step guide for your car, a parts and tools list, and the confidence to handle it next time.

Can I bring a friend or family member?

Absolutely. The more the merrier — no extra charge for observers, and two people learning is even better than one.
